# Outside

A Typora theme family with three variants that share the same typographic system:

- **Outside** — a clean white theme. English uses the serif typeface *Source Serif 4*, Chinese uses *Source Han Serif* (Song style), with a blue accent.
- **Outside Paper** — a warm paper theme. English uses the rounded *Josefin Sans*, Chinese uses rounded typefaces, with sunset-red / sunset-purple accents on a warm paper background.
- **Outside Dark** — a full dark theme with a completely darkened Typora UI (sidebar, title bar, menus, dialogs), with a blue accent.

All fonts are bundled with the theme and are open-source under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). Designed and tested on Windows.

## Install

1. Download the latest `outside-theme.zip` from the [releases page](https://github.com/Chenyi-X/typora-outside-theme/releases).
2. Open Typora → `Preferences` → `Appearance` → `Open Theme Folder`.
3. Copy everything from the zip into that folder.
4. Restart Typora and choose **Outside**, **Outside Paper**, or **Outside Dark**.
