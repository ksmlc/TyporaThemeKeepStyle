A cozy Animal Crossing–inspired theme with warm parchment surfaces, mint accents, and soft 3D pillow shadows. Includes a **Light** version and a **Dark** (forest night) variant.

Highlights:

- Six distinct heading styles — gradient cards, color bars, capsule stickers — every level recognisable at a glance.
- Paw-print horizontal rules and custom 3D checkboxes.
- High-contrast Source Mode: Markdown markers tinted mint, titles in cream — no more illegible default blue on dark backgrounds.
